This is a place to store jobs that I have already looked at, saved, eliminated, applied for, etc. Any potential job should be determined not only by qualifications, but by the following Q&A.

Q: What are you looking for in your next role? What would you like to avoid? (Might include aspects like technologies you want to work with, industries you’re interested in, team dynamics, software development practices … whatever is important to you) *

A: In the past I have worked on teams that, by default, pair-programmed on every card in the backlog. These last few years at F5 has made me miss team collaboration and I strongly desire to go back to working with others on problems and shipping tangible features. Currently, I rarely collaborate with others and am very siloed. Pairing on every card is not necessarily something I would want to do again, however.

Less importantly, but still a strong wish, is to work on a product that relates to my personal life in some way, whether it be IoT, Type 1 Diabetes, home improvement, recreational outdoor activities or many other things. Or a product that has a visible and profound positive effect on people. Not a product which is simply framed as helping others like so many of them. An example from my past experience would be something like Traverse which helped the case file workers manage their case files which meant they could better serve the people and be more effective in their role. An example of something I do not find relatable is private aircraft maintenance.

Finally, I would like to work at a company that pays for extracurricular activities, meaning conferences relating to the product and/or relating to software. The few conferences that I have been allowed to attend have been immensely valuable to me and the company. I often bring back fresh perspective and share the knowledge that I have gained with my team.
